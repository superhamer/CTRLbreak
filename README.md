# CTRL Break

**A calmer way to survive the workday.**

CTRL Break turns one spare minute into a real reset. It schedules short desk-stretch breaks around your working hours and delivers them as gentle reminders. Tap one and a full-screen break begins: a peaceful scene, a clear stretch guide, soft ambient sound, and a short quote to reflect on while you move.

Built for people who sit too much and forget to stand up.

---

## Highlights

- **Breaks that fit your workday** — reminders arrive around your own working hours, on the days you choose.
- **One tap and you're stretching** — a reminder takes you straight into a calm, one-minute break.
- **Made to be looked forward to** — peaceful visuals, soft ambience, and a thought to carry into the rest of your day.
- **Built for building a habit** — gentle accountability when you miss a break, and a simple record of the streak you're creating.

## How It Works

1. Create a schedule — days, hours, and how often you want a break.
2. Go about your day.

## Getting Started

## Privacy

CTRL Break keeps everything on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, and no tracking. Your schedules, preferences, and break history never leave your phone.

## FAQ

**Does it work when the app is closed?**
Yes. Reminders are delivered through iOS notifications and tap straight into your break.

**Can I have different schedules for different routines?**
Yes — create as many schedules as you like, each with its own days, hours, interval, and on/off switch.

**Is my data private?**
Completely. Everything stays on your device.

## Disclaimer

CTRL Break offers general wellness suggestions for healthy adults and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
