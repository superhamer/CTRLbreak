What is CTRL Break?
CTRL Break is a wellness app that helps office workers build a sustainable stretching habit. It schedules short, guided desk-stretch breaks throughout your workday and delivers them through a calm, full-screen break experience designed to take exactly one minute.
How do the breaks work?
You create your own schedules — choose which days, the start and end of your working window, and how often you want a break. During those hours, CTRL Break reminds you at your chosen interval. Tap the reminder and the break begins instantly, whether you opened it from a notification or launched the app yourself.
What happens during a break?
Each break is a one-minute experience: a peaceful animated scene, an illustrated stretch guide, soft ambient sound, and a short quote to reflect on while you stretch. When the minute is up, you're gently returned to your day.
Can I choose which stretches I get?
Yes. You can let CTRL Break surprise you with a random stretch each break, or hand-pick the stretches you want in your rotation. There are 12 desk-friendly stretches covering neck, shoulders, back, hips, wrists, and legs.
What makes the reminders different from a regular timer?
CTRL Break is built around real work schedules — weekday-aware, with multiple named schedules for different routines. And it keeps you accountable: if you miss a reminder, it can send you one gentle follow-up nudge a few minutes later. Start the break, and the nudge disappears.
Is the app customizable?
Yes — schedules, break frequency, stretch selection, missed-break reminders, and the break experience itself are all configurable. The goal is a habit that fits your day, not the other way around.
Does CTRL Break work when the app is closed?
Yes. Reminders are delivered through iOS notifications, so they arrive even when the app isn't running. Tapping a reminder takes you straight into your break — no digging through menus.
Is my data private?
All of your schedules, preferences, and break history stay on your device. CTRL Break has no accounts, no sign-up, and no tracking.
Is this medical advice?
No. The stretches in CTRL Break are general wellness suggestions for healthy adults and are not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have an injury, pain, or a health condition, consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting any stretching routine. Stop immediately if you feel pain or discomfort.
